# Agency Project Lifecycle — AI Opportunity Analysis

An analysis of the standard design-and-build project flow used by most agencies (including Elixirr Digital), mapped phase-by-phase against current AI and tooling opportunities. The goal: identify where existing workflows leak time, quality, or margin — and where new tooling can close those gaps.

## Scope

The lifecycle covered runs from **pre-sale estimation** through **client handover and support**. This is deliberately broader than the existing [AI-Augmented Dev Pipeline](/reports/ai-augmented-dev-pipeline/) and [Dark Factory](/reports/dark-factory/) reports, which focus primarily on the build phase. Here we examine the full chain:

1. Estimation and scoping
2. Briefing and discovery
3. Information architecture and content planning
4. Design (UX/UI)
5. Technical specification and planning
6. Build (development)
7. Content population and migration
8. QA and testing
9. Client training
10. Delivery (launch)
11. Post-launch support and governance
